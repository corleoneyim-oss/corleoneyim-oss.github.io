Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Google Drive access

COIN Research requests the Google Drive OAuth scope, which can authorize access across your Drive. The Windows application is configured to operate only on receipts in its designated private research folder. That folder restriction is enforced by the application, not by the OAuth scope.

Use and storage

The application lists matching receipt names, creates receipts and reads their original bytes by file ID to check integrity and recover interrupted work. Task identifiers, receipt contents and execution records are stored in the local research workspace and, where applicable, in the private Drive folder. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in a restricted local Windows user directory and are used to obtain access tokens from Google. Evidence expressly selected for engineering review may be retained in private project storage. OAuth credentials are excluded from these records.

Google user data is used to provide this receipt workflow. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. The application's use of Google API data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

You can revoke the application’s access through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access and does not delete existing local records or private Drive files. Those are retained until removed by the owner.

This informational site uses no analytics scripts or cookies of its own. Its hosting provider may process standard web request information.
